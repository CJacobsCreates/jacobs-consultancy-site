02 // Cloud Infrastructure

Solid foundations, anywhere in the world.

Old shared hosting, a server in a cupboard, or a cloud account that grew without a plan. It works until traffic spikes, a disk fills up or nobody remembers how it was set up.

What we do
  • We design and run cloud setups on AWS and Cloudflare: hosting and servers, storage and databases, networking and DNS.
  • Global delivery through a CDN (Amazon CloudFront or Cloudflare's edge). Your site loads from servers close to each visitor, wherever they are.
  • Deployment pipelines: each update is built, tested and deployed automatically, with separate staging and live environments, one-click releases and easy rollback.
  • Scaling, backups and monitoring, so busy periods don't take you offline and problems get caught early.
  • Admin dashboards with role-based access, plus one-click exports (CSV, Excel, PDF) for management and accounting.
  • Migrations from on-site servers or shared hosting, planned to avoid downtime.
What you get
A documented, secure setup built on AWS best practice that scales when you need it.
  • Hosting, storage and databases sized for what you need now
  • Automatic backups and monitoring from day one
  • Fast loading worldwide through a CDN
  • Documentation and access you own

05 // Modernization

Stuck on old legacy systems?

A WordPress site built around 2010 and held together by ancient plugins. A server in a back room that nobody wants to touch. Software only one person knows how to run. It still works, mostly, but every year it gets harder, riskier and more expensive to keep alive.

What we do
  • We map what you have: old servers, legacy software, outdated websites and the data inside them.
  • We upgrade or rebuild legacy software on a modern, supported setup, keeping what already works.
  • We migrate you off old on-site servers and shared hosting to the cloud, with your data moved across intact and checked.
  • We refresh or rebuild stale websites, replacing ancient plugins with maintained ones.
  • We cut over in phases, running old and new side by side, so there's no downtime and nothing gets lost.
What you get
Systems that are supported, documented and easy to hand over, without a risky all-at-once switch.
  • A modern, supported setup in place of the old one
  • Your data moved across intact and verified
  • A phased cutover with no downtime
  • Documentation, so it no longer lives in one person's head

06 // Cost Optimization

Pay for what you actually use.

What we do
  • We review your cloud, hosting and software bills line by line.
  • We rightsize oversized servers and databases, and switch off what isn't used.
  • We consolidate overlapping tools and licences.
  • We bring old systems up to date and move them to fit-for-purpose setups, such as Cloudflare or AWS, where that makes sense.
  • Budgets, alerts and ongoing cost monitoring keep the bill from creeping back.
What you get
A clear picture of where the money goes, a plan built around the savings we find, and monitoring so it stays that way.

08 // Security

Know who can see what.

Shared passwords, files in personal drives, nobody quite sure who has access to what, and backups no one has tested. Usually it's fine, until it isn't.

What we do
  • We decide where your data should live and set up secure cloud storage following AWS best practice.
  • Access control with least-privilege accounts and multi-factor sign-in: everyone gets only what their role needs.
  • Encryption in transit and at rest, plus backups with tested recovery.
  • Hardened websites and apps, with security headers, updates and monitoring.
  • POPIA-aware handling of personal information.
What you get
Clear rules for who can see what, data stored properly and backed up, and a plan if something goes wrong.
  • Access by role, with multi-factor sign-in
  • Encrypted data and backups with tested recovery
  • Security headers, updates and monitoring
  • POPIA-aware handling of personal information
