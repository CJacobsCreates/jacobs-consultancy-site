01 // What we do

Everything a store needs before the first order.

Most new stores lose sales to small things: a confusing menu, the wrong shipping rate, a product page that doesn't answer the obvious question. We set up the whole store, not just the theme.

New store builds

A complete Shopify store for a small or medium brand, from an empty account to taking orders: theme, pages, navigation, policies and a checkout you've tested yourself.

  • New brandsYour first online store, set up the way it should be
  • Growing shopsA rebuild when the current store no longer fits the catalogue

Products & collections

Products with proper variants, photos, SKUs and stock, grouped into collections and filters that match how your customers shop.

Theme customisation

A theme styled to your brand: type, colour, layout and product pages that read well on a phone, where most of your customers will be.

Payments, shipping & tax

Payment methods, shipping zones and rates, and tax settings for the places you sell to, checked with real test orders before launch.

Migrations

Moving from WooCommerce, Wix, Squarespace or another platform. We bring over products, customers and order history where the platform allows, and redirect old links so you keep your search traffic.

Ongoing store care

Once you're live, we stay on. One person who knows your store, handles the updates and fixes, and uploads new products when you're busy running the business.

  • Monthly theme and app updates
  • Product uploads and edits
  • Fixes when something breaks
  • Direct contact, no ticket queue
