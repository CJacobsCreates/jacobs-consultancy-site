    01 // What we build

    Systems built around how you operate.

    Every organisation runs differently, so nothing here is off the shelf. We start from your processes and the systems you already have.

    Real-time operations dashboards

    See where your vehicles, equipment, stock and people are, and what's happening across sites, live. We use the data you already have, from trackers and sensors to booking and stock systems.

    • HospitalsEquipment location, bed status and maintenance schedules
    • Mining · buildingVehicles and machinery, from the trackers already fitted
    • Retail · factoriesStock levels and production output across sites

    SOP & operations platforms

    Your procedures, checklists and compliance sign-offs, digitised. Everyone follows the same steps, and you get a full audit trail of who did what, and when.

    • HealthcareShift handovers, cleaning and equipment checks
    • On sitePre-start checks and safety inspections
    • RetailOpening, closing and quality checks

    Operations analytics & reporting

    KPIs, trends and scheduled reports built from your operational data, so managers see problems early instead of in next month's report.

    API & plugin integrations

    We connect what you already run, like accounting, HR, booking, stock, CRM and tracker platforms, through APIs, webhooks and custom plugins, with data syncing between them so it is entered once.

    Cloud infrastructure & DevOps

    Cloud setups on AWS and Cloudflare built on AWS best practice: fast global delivery, deployment pipelines, scaling, backups, monitoring and incident response.

    Security & data storage done properly

    Data stored in the right place, least-privilege access, encryption, tested backups and POPIA-aware handling of personal information.

    Cost optimization

    Your cloud, software and IT spend reviewed line by line: rightsizing, cutting unused services and licences, and monitoring so costs stay down.

    Staff & client portals

    Admin dashboards with role-based access, self-service portals and one-click exports (CSV, Excel, PDF) for management and accounting.

    Outsourced Head of Technology

    One point of contact for anything tech or online, like having a tech lead in-house without the full-time hire. You get someone who knows your systems, plans ahead and is accountable when something breaks.

    • Technology roadmap and budgeting
    • Vendor, licence and supplier management
    • Oversight of websites, systems, cloud and security
    • A regular check-in, plus direct contact when you need it
